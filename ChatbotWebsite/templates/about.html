About Soulmate

Your dedicated mental health companion designed to provide reliable information, resources, and support.

Our Commitment

We understand that seeking help for mental health concerns can be challenging. Our chatbot is designed to make the process as comfortable, accessible, and anonymous as possible.

Important Notice: While SoulMate is here to support you, our chatbot is not a substitute for professional help. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please seek immediate help from a mental health professional.

Our Mission

We believe that education and awareness are critical components of mental health. Our chatbot aims to provide accurate and up-to-date information on various mental health conditions, including symptoms, causes, and effects.

We also provide a variety of resources, such as mental health tests and mindfulness exercises, to help you evaluate your mental health and improve your overall wellbeing.

Our Team

SoulMate was developed by dedicated students passionate about mental health. We are committed to playing a part in breaking down the stigma surrounding mental health and creating a supportive community.
